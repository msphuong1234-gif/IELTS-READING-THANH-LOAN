IELTS Traps

Specific-detail trap • Paraphrase trap • Opinion polarity • Direction-of-change • Added-detail trap • Indirect causal-chain trap

Lessons Learned

  1. NOT GIVEN: không suy từ “many factors” sang một factor cụ thể.
  2. prevent có thể được paraphrase bằng “without succumbing to”.
  3. favourable appraisal đối lập với disagrees.
  4. population growth đối lập với population reduction.
  5. drank beer không đồng nghĩa made beer at home.
  6. Indirect cause có thể thể hiện qua chuỗi A → B → C.

Quick Review

necessary but not sufficient → required yet inadequate on its own
antiseptic properties → ability to inhibit harmful microorganisms
a favourable appraisal → an approving review
a burst in population growth → a sudden rise in the number of people
a tax was introduced on malt → the authorities began taxing malt
