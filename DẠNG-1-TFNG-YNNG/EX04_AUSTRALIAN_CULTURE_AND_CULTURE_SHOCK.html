Lessons Learned

  1. options ↔ alternatives.
  2. Cùng có circumstances chưa đủ: kiểm tra chủ thể/hành động.
  3. first names minh họa equality/status.
  4. critical đối lập positive comments.
  5. only discussed with close friends trái people you do not know well.
  6. Older people không tạo ra phép so sánh younger vs older.

Quick Review

give a number of options → offer several possible courses of action
work out which one is the best → decide which option is most suitable
uncomfortable with differences in status → uneasy about social hierarchy
call each other by their first names → address one another using given names
financial matters → topics relating to money
friendship firmly established → relationship close and secure
