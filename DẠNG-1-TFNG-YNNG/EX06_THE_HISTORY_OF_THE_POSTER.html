Lessons Learned

  1. as common as illustration = phổ biến ngang tranh minh họa, không phải more widely seen than.
  2. continues to exert its influence today ↔ đặc điểm phong cách vẫn hiện diện trong thiết kế hiện đại.
  3. post-war marketplace + international identification + universal solutions hỗ trợ ý “met a global need at a particular time”.
  4. teacher in Basel không cho phép suy ra students là nguồn ý tưởng.
  5. NOT GIVEN: thiếu đúng quan hệ nghĩa dù có keyword giống nhau.
  6. TFNG phải kiểm tra mức độ, thời gian, chủ thể và quan hệ nghĩa.

Quick Review

photography became as common as illustration → photos were used about as often as illustrations
continues to exert its influence today → still shapes design in the present day
a strong demand for clarity → a substantial need for clear communication
international identification → recognition across different countries
universal solutions → approaches usable across cultures
complex and chaotic, playful and spontaneous → intricate and disorderly, lively and unplanned
